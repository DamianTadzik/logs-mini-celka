# Test purpose

The purpose of the tests was to try flying with RPI, onboard, with canlogger moved to the front, and with smaller battery.

Secondnary target was to try out an automatic controller, and manual controller written in python.

Another goal was to collect 6dof imu data, and generally to collect data, Ideally i wanted to collect data from manual flights in order to teach some NN model.

# Outcome

The hot glue gave up before i arrived, the RPI and Logger were not attached to the hull 
That makes the results unusable, due to the fact that RPI and the logger has significant mass which was deatached from the hull

Automatic controller needs a lot of tuning, maybe latencies in the system should be reduced. 

I couldnt get stable manual flight with that setup (probbably multiple causes of that)
